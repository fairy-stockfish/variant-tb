# Cambodian chess

## Rules

Cambodian chess (Ouk Chatrang) as implemented by Fairy-Stockfish's
`cambodian` variant: pieces, pawns and promotion are as in
[Makruk](makruk.md). The special first moves of king and met are not
supported: the tables assume that these rights are gone, like Syzygy
assumes that castling is no longer possible. An engine must not probe while
they are still available.

Counting (Fairy-Stockfish `CAMBODIAN_COUNTING`) differs from Makruk:

* Board's honour (63 moves = 126 plies, count starting at 0) starts as soon
  as the side to move has at most two pieces besides its king, **also with
  pawns on the board**. Up to 5 pieces this is always the case, so the
  tables assume that the board's honour count is running in every position
  that is not under pieces' honour. Pawn moves do not reset the count.
* Pieces' honour needs a bare king and no pawns; the limits are 7, 15, 21,
  31, 43 and 63 moves (count starting at 2n plies).
* A fresh count starts (Fairy-Stockfish `do_move()`) when, after a capture
  or promotion, the side to move has a bare king and no pawns are left, or
  when a bare king captures the last pawn. Any other move continues the
  count.
* The game is drawn as soon as `countingPly > countingLimit`, unless the
  side to move is checkmated. Stalemate is a draw, there is no 50-move
  rule.

## Building and usage

`make -C src cambodian` builds `cmtbgen`, `cmtbver`, `cmtbprobe` and
`tbcheck`. All tables, also those with pawns, are generated by `cmtbgen` and
verified by `cmtbver` (there is no `cmtbgenp`). Files have the suffixes
`.cmw`/`.cmz`; subtables are looked up in `$CMTBPATH`, verification logs go
to `cmtblog.txt`. Table names and the helper scripts
(`src/run_makruk.py --variant cambodian`, `src/mktest.py --variant
cambodian`) are as for [Makruk](makruk.md).

## File format

WDL magic `0x91b3c6d2`, DTZ magic `0x4e7a0f18`. Because the count also runs
with pawns, **all** Cambodian tables store a WDL value for a fresh count
and the counting distance `D`, with the raw index and the probing rules of
the [Makruk pawnless tables](makruk.md#pawnless-tables-krsvkn-kmmvk-),
with these differences:

* Tables with pawns are indexed like tables with a khon (left-right
  mirroring only). Pawn moves and promotions are ordinary moves for `D`.
* Fresh budget: 127 plies, or `2L - 2n + 1` with a bare king and no pawns.
* A move that starts a fresh count after which the mover loses (e.g. a bare
  king capturing the last pawn into a lost pieces' honour ending) loses at
  once: it counts as `D = 0` for the mover, like a mate. The 1-ply search
  for the side not stored in the DTZ file (`probe_dtz_makruk()` in
  `src/probe.c`) also generates pawn moves and promotions.

An engine compares `D` with `countingLimit - countingPly + 1` in every
position. If `countStarted` is used to start the count manually, the values
of `counting_limit(countStarted)` and `counting_ply(countStarted)` should be
used; the switch to a shorter board's honour count
(`board_honor_counting_shorter`) is not modelled by the tables.

## Resources

Up to 4 pieces (measured, 4 cores): 35 tables, 4.5 CPU minutes for
generation and 2 for verification, 13 MB. The 5-piece tables were not
generated; since all of them, including the 50 with pawns, use the full raw
index, an estimated 12–16 hours on 4 cores including verification,
2 GB RAM and 5–7 GB of disk space are needed.

## Limitations

* Assumes a running board's honour count (see above), which holds in games
  but not necessarily for arbitrary FEN positions.
* Tested: all tables up to 4 pieces pass `cmtbver` and `mktest.py`. 5-piece
  tables are untested.

## Tables and probing

No tables are published yet, and Fairy-Stockfish does not probe them yet;
see [Makruk](makruk.md#probing-in-an-engine).
